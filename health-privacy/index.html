Health Dashboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Health Dashboard is a private, single-person app. Its only user is the person who built it and runs it, viewing their own health data. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, the app reads weight and body-fat measurements from the owner's own Google Health account, using a read-only Google permission (googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly). It cannot change or delete anything in that account.

Where the data goes

  • Measurements are stored on a private server the owner operates, and shown only on a dashboard that requires the owner's Google sign-in.
  • The data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, and is not used for advertising.
  • Summaries may be sent to an AI service only when the owner asks the dashboard a question, and only as aggregates the owner can see.

Google API data

Health Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored data is deleted on request by the owner.

Contact

Questions go to the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
