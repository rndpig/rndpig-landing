# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

rndpig (the owner) and a handful of allowlisted family/friends. They land here on
phone or desktop to jump into one of the rndpig.com apps — it's a personal
launcher, not a marketing page. Traffic is near-zero by design; the audience is
the builder himself.

## Product Purpose

rndpig.com is the compact portfolio view of every deployed app in the rndpig
Coding Projects portfolio (deer, lawn, weather, home, network, dinner, dept56).
Success = every live app has a card, the page loads instantly, and it works
one-handed on a phone. It must be updated whenever a new app ships (standing
step in the new-app-bootstrap workflow).

## Brand Personality

Personal, home-grown, quietly playful. The identity centers on the **rndpig
handle** — expressed as a pig **snout mark** (ellipse + two nostrils), never a
pig emoji. Think "well-made tool wall in a home workshop": tidy, warm, a wink
of humor, zero corporate gloss.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing-page grammar: hero metrics, gradient text, uppercase eyebrow
  labels, glassmorphism, marketing copy.
- Emoji-as-logo (the old admin 🐷) — the snout mark is the brand.
- Loud color drench; the pink is an accent on a calm dark ground, not a paint job.

## Design Principles

1. **The snout is the brand** — one mark, everywhere (header, favicon, admin).
2. **Launcher first** — every design choice serves "find app, tap, gone" in
   under 3 seconds, especially on mobile.
3. **Compact over impressive** — no hero, no scroll theater; the grid IS the page.
4. **Personal warmth, engineered restraint** — the pig-pink lives ONLY in the
   snout logo (owner decision 2026-07-04); interactive UI uses a calm blue
   accent on a cool dark ground, with friendly rounded type.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on the dark ground (verify the pink accent at link size).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored — entrance motion collapses to instant/fade.
- Touch targets ≥ 44px on mobile; whole card is the tap target.
- No JS required for any content or navigation.
